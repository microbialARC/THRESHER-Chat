# THRESHER Phylothreshold — Concept Guide

> What a phylothreshold is, how candidate phylothresholds are scanned within each hierarchical
> clustering group, what is recorded at every candidate, and how the final phylothreshold is
> chosen from them.

## What a phylothreshold is

Phylothresholds are SNP thresholds corrected for phylogenetic topology and inferred directly from
the genomic structure of each dataset. A phylothreshold is therefore determined by two key
components: SNP distances and the group phylogeny.

THRESHER does not impose a predefined value. It evaluates a range of candidate phylothresholds
and determines the final phylothreshold from among them as the one that best captures the
underlying phylogenetic structure.

## Candidate phylothresholds and the scan range

Multiple candidate phylothresholds are generated during the candidate threshold scan. The scan is
bounded by the observed data, where the floor is the larger of $\tau_{\text{floor}}$ and
the smallest study–study SNP distance, and the ceiling is the smaller of $\tau_{\text{ceil}}$
(default 500) and the largest study–study SNP distance. No cutoff below the closest study pair or
above the most distant study pair is tested:

$$\tau_{\text{floor}}^{\,k} = \max\!\Big( \tau_{\text{floor}},\ \big\lceil \min_{i \neq j} d(g_i, g_j) \big\rceil \Big), \qquad
\tau_{\text{ceil}}^{\,k} = \min\!\Big( \tau_{\text{ceil}},\ \big\lfloor \max_{i \neq j} d(g_i, g_j) \big\rfloor \Big)$$

The superscript $k$ indexes the hierarchical clustering group $H_k$: both bounds are derived from
the SNP distances within that group and therefore differ between groups.


## Strain composition at each candidate

Every candidate phylothreshold carries a strain composition. Single-linkage clustering,
phylogenetic correction, and CladeBreaker together produce, at every candidate $\tau$, a complete
assignment of the group's study genomes to strains, the strain composition $\mathcal{S}(\tau)$:

$$\mathcal{S}(\tau) = \{\, S_1, \ldots, S_{m_\tau} \,\}, \qquad \bigcup_{S \in \mathcal{S}(\tau)} S = HG$$

Each strain $S \in \mathcal{S}(\tau)$ carries three attributes:

- **Category**:clone or singleton.
- **Correction flag**: whether its composition was modified by phylogenetic correction or
  CladeBreaker, rather than set by SNP single linkage alone.
- **Bootstrap support** $b(S)$: the support of the strain's clade for a clone, and zero for a
  singleton.

When no group phylogeny is available, the correction flag and bootstrap support are recorded as
missing.

## The per-threshold record and the group database

CORE records the strain composition together with summary statistics for every $\tau$ in the
scan range. The per-threshold record is

$$R(\tau) = \big(\, \tau,\ \delta(\tau),\ n^{\text{pre}}_{\text{clone}}(\tau),\ n^{\text{pre}}_{\text{single}}(\tau),\ n_{\text{corr}}(\tau),\ n^{\text{post}}_{\text{clone}}(\tau),\ n^{\text{post}}_{\text{single}}(\tau),\ \mathcal{S}(\tau) \,\big)$$

where $\delta$ is the discrepancy, $n_{\text{corr}}$ the number of accepted phylogenetic
corrections, and the pre- and post-correction counts are those produced before and after
correction and CladeBreaker. Scanning the full range gives the group's intermediate database,

$$\mathcal{D}_k = \{\, R(\tau) : \tau = \tau_{\text{floor}}^{\,k}, \ldots, \tau_{\text{ceil}}^{\,k} \,\},$$

the strain composition of the group at every candidate phylothreshold together with its
summaries. A group resolved without a scan contributes a single all-singleton record.

For each group, $\mathcal{D}_k$ is visualized in a Multi-SNP-Threshold Plot (MSTP), written to
`thresher/output/MSTP/Group{Group_ID}_MSTP.pdf` in the run's output directory. The MSTP traces the
clone count, the singleton count, and the discrepancy across the candidate phylothresholds, with
the mean and median bootstrap support of all clones in the group shown in the upper panel.


## From candidates to the final phylothreshold

From $\mathcal{D}_k$, each of the four currently supported endpoint methods selects the group's
final phylothreshold, and with it the strain composition reported for the group. The final
phylothreshold is therefore always determined under a specific endpoint method, and the same
hierarchical clustering group can have up to four different phylothresholds — one to four
distinct values, depending on how many endpoint methods arrive at the same one.


## Implementation

Every candidate phylothreshold and its strain composition are generated by `thresher_input.R` and
saved to `thresher/input/thresher_input.RDS` by the Snakemake rule `thresher_input`. The rule
takes the hierarchical clustering groups, the study SNP matrix, the study–public SNP matrix, and
the group phylogenies as input, with the threshold floor, threshold ceiling, singleton
threshold, correction bootstrap, and CladeBreaker setting as parameters. The script reads the
scan range from those parameters:

```r
threshold_floor <- snakemake@params[["threshold_floor"]]
threshold_floor <- as.integer(threshold_floor)
threshold_ceiling <- snakemake@params[["threshold_ceiling"]]
threshold_ceiling <- as.integer(threshold_ceiling)
```

Groups are processed in parallel with `mclapply`. Within each group:

1. **Overlimit genomes** are excluded from the scan. If no genome remains, the group returns
   early with every genome as a singleton and no cutoff.
2. **The group phylogeny** (`Group{Group_ID}.contree`) exists only for groups of at least four
   genomes, since bootstrap analysis is not meaningful below four sequences; without it, no
   phylogenetic correction is applied. When present, the tree is midpoint-rooted and each
   internal node is summarized with its bootstrap support (the root labeled `Root`) and its
   descendant genomes.
3. **The effective floor** is set to the larger of the user-defined floor and the smallest SNP
   distance in the group. If that floor already reaches the singleton threshold, the group
   returns early with all genomes as singletons, and no phylogenetic correction is applied.
4. **Otherwise, every cutoff in the range is scanned:** single-linkage clustering, phylogenetic
   correction under the correction bootstrap, CladeBreaker when enabled, and resolution of
   overlapping corrected strains, followed by the post-correction counts, the strain
   composition, and the mean and median bootstrap support over clones. Without a group
   phylogeny, the correction-dependent fields are recorded as missing.

Each record holds `HC_group`, `cutoff`, `discrepancy`, `before_correction_singletons`,
`before_correction_clones`, `clones_corrected`, `after_correction_singletons`,
`after_correction_clones`, `median_strain_bootstrap_support`, `mean_strain_bootstrap_support`, and
`strain_composition`, where each strain in the composition carries its `strain_id`, `category`,
`genome`, `correction` flag, and `bootstrap_support`.
